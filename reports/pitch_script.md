# Three-minute pitch script

## 0:00-0:35 - One disease, two molecular failures

This child's MVA is best explained by two BUB1B variants. The first, Leu737Ter,
is a premature UGA stop expected to trigger nonsense-mediated decay. The second,
Asn1002Lys, is an ultra-rare missense change in the C-terminal kinase region.
Both calls are high quality. They fit a null-plus-hypomorphic recessive model,
although trans phase still needs parental or long-molecule confirmation.

That gives us a precise question. Can we recover useful BUBR1 protein from either
allele, and can we do it without helping genetically unstable cells survive?

## 0:35-1:20 - Match one approved medicine to each failure

For the stop allele, we test gentamicin. It is an approved aminoglycoside with
human proof-of-concept for premature-stop readthrough, but results in other
diseases have been variable. The exact BUB1B context is UGA followed by A, and
the transcript may be depleted by NMD, so we measure allele-specific RNA first.
No transcript or no full-length protein at reachable exposure means no-go.

For the missense allele, we test arimoclomol. Patient-cell research showed that
some BUBR1 kinase-region missense proteins turn over rapidly and depend on
chaperones for stability. Arimoclomol is FDA-approved for Niemann-Pick disease C
and amplifies a stress-induced heat-shock response. But nobody has shown that it
rescues BUB1B or Asn1002Lys. That is exactly what our experiment is designed to
falsify.

## 1:20-2:00 - A downstream candidate with a hard safety contradiction

We also test sirolimus as a downstream benchmark. In a 2026 fly model of
spindle-checkpoint-loss MVA-like microcephaly, rapamycin feeding improved neural
stem-cell survival by boosting autophagy. But sirolimus does not repair the
checkpoint, and its immunosuppression warning is a profound concern in a
cancer-predisposition syndrome.

So our endpoint is not simply more surviving cells. We use longitudinal live
imaging and single-cell copy-number profiling to ask whether treatment prevents
new segregation errors or merely preserves abnormal clones.

## 2:00-2:42 - The experiment

We build patient-derived and CRISPR-isogenic cells carrying each allele alone,
both alleles, a corrected control, and wild type. We test each medicine alone
before any combination. Advancement requires direct target engagement, restored
BUBR1 abundance or checkpoint function, fewer new missegregation events, activity
near approved human exposure, and no clonogenic or tumor-surveillance penalty.

The central result must reproduce across two models, two orthogonal assays, and
an independent laboratory. A generic stress response or better cell growth is
not enough.

## 2:42-3:00 - Why this matters

This is not a cure claim. It is a disciplined route from this child's exact
alleles to three approved-drug hypotheses with pre-registered stop rules. The
same null-plus-unstable-missense framework can scale to other recessive rare
diseases, while the rescue-versus-cancer index tackles the central safety problem
of chromosomal-instability syndromes.
